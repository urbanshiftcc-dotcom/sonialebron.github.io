Are the documents really specific to NJ and NY?+
Yes. The handbook was written specifically for private households operating in New Jersey and New York. It includes full state supplements for both states, covering the Domestic Workers' Bill of Rights, Earned Sick Leave, Family Leave Insurance, NJFLA, NY Paid Family Leave, NYC Earned Safe and Sick Time, and NY Paid Prenatal Leave. These are not generic templates adapted for the region. They were built for it.
Can I use the handbook for multiple employees?+
Yes. Your purchase licenses you to use and edit the document for all domestic employees within your household. The license covers a single household. It does not permit resale, redistribution, or use by a staffing agency, family office, or third party on behalf of multiple principals. A separate commercial license is required for those purposes.

Immediately. All digital products are delivered via Payhip within seconds of purchase. You will receive a download link by email. The handbook is a fully editable PDF file that opens in Adobe Acrobat, Microsoft Edge, or any PDF viewer on any device.
Can the audit really be completed in 48 hours?+
Not always. Timelines vary by scope. The 48-hour audit is designed for a single-property household with a contained headcount and manageable vendor relationships. Larger or more complex households, with multiple properties, larger staff, or extensive contractor networks, require more time to audit properly. All engagements are scoped during a confidential pre-engagement conversation, and the timeline is confirmed before any commitment is made.
Is the audit confidential?+
Completely. All audit engagements are covered by a mutual non-disclosure agreement signed before commencement. It is a core value.

A retained engagement means Vorton Advisory becomes your ongoing private household advisor. Yes. Travel within the tri-state area is included in audit engagements. Travel costs for engagements outside the tri-state area are discussed and confirmed during the scoping conversation.
